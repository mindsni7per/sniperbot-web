2. Information we collect

We only collect what the Bot needs to do its job. Depending on which features a server uses, this can include:

  • Account identifiers: your Discord user ID, username and display name, and the server IDs you share with the Bot.
  • Activity counts: the number of messages you send in servers where the Bot is active. We store the count, not the content of your regular messages.
  • Invite data: which member invited which new member, based on invite use counts.
  • Vouches: the vouch giver, recipient, reason, optional item, and timestamp.
  • Payment proofs: the customer, the item, the payment method, the amount, any note, a screenshot link if one was posted, and who handled the sale. The Bot records these details but does not process payments.
  • Tickets: ticket channel names, the member who opened the ticket, claim and close details, and the answers given to ticket questions. When a ticket closes, the full message history of the ticket is saved as a transcript.
  • Giveaways, wheels and applications: entries, winners, and the answers given in staff applications and leave of absence requests.
  • Moderation records: bans, kicks, mutes, warnings, the reason given, the staff member involved, and timestamps.
  • Dashboard login data: when you log in with Discord, we receive your user ID, username, avatar, and the list of servers you are in, which we use only to check whether you have Manage Server permission. We do not request your email address.

We do not read your private (direct) messages to the Bot except when you message it directly to use a feature, such as a leave of absence request or an application.

3. How we use information

  • To run commands, tickets, vouches, giveaways, moderation, applications and other features.
  • To show statistics, leaderboards and dashboard pages to server staff.
  • To keep servers safe, including detecting abuse and enforcing our Terms.
  • To fix bugs and improve the Bot.
  • To respond to support requests and legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Who controls the data

Server owners and staff decide how the Bot is used in their server, including which features are turned on, what is recorded, and who can see it. The server owner is responsible for telling members what data their server collects, and for handling requests about that data within their server. We process the data on the server's behalf and for the purposes above.

5. Dashboard access

The dashboard shows data from the server it is set up for. Only people with Manage Server or Administrator permission in that server can open it. Staff can see transcripts, moderation logs, vouch and message totals, and panel settings, so they should follow their server's rules when handling this information.

6. Sharing

We share information only in these cases:

  • Service providers: companies that host the Bot and dashboard, under contracts that limit their use of the data.
  • Discord: data is sent to Discord as needed to run the Bot, as described in Discord's policies.
  • Legal reasons: when required by law, a court order, or to protect people's safety.
  • Server staff: the data is visible to staff in the servers where the feature is used.

7. How long we keep information

Information is kept while the server uses the Bot and the feature that needs it. Message counts older than 60 days are removed from daily history, but totals are kept. Transcripts and logs stay until a staff member deletes them or we delete them on request. Account data for dashboard logins is held only in your session and is cleared when you log out or the session expires.

If the Bot is removed from your server, server staff can ask us in writing to delete that server's data. We will do so within [NUMBER] days unless the law requires us to keep it longer.

  • Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA, including objecting to processing or complaining to a regulator. We will respond within the time required by law.

9. Security

No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Do not share your bot token, client secret, or passwords with anyone.
